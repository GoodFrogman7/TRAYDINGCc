## Regime view

Macro-led bear trend with high realized volatility. BTC is about 50% off its October 2025 high, has lost six weeks running, and trades below its 200-week average while the Fed tightens and long yields sit near 5.1%. The last three sessions look like post-capitulation basing ($62K-$65K) with sentiment at extreme fear and the first ETF inflow in eight sessions, which leaves room for a sharp countertrend bounce. Until $68K-$70K is reclaimed that bounce is a rally inside a downtrend, not a trend change; a daily close through $60K-$62K reopens the July low ($57,718) and the $50Ks that Wintermute flagged.
